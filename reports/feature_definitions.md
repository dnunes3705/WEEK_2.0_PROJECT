# Feature Definitions (for Hypothesis Testing)

These engineered features are generated by `python src/build_features.py` from `data/raw/foodhub_order.csv`.

Important limitation: the dataset contains no timestamps, so *retention* and *repeat within time window* cannot be computed as defined in `problem_definition.txt`. The features below provide **engagement proxies within the observed dataset**.

## Order-level (`data/processed/orders_features.csv`)

- `is_weekend`: `day_of_the_week == "Weekend"`.
- `rated_flag`: `rating != "Not given"`.
- `rating_num`: numeric rating (3–5) when `rated_flag==True`, else missing.
- `total_fulfillment_time_min`: `food_preparation_time + delivery_time`.
- `delivery_share_of_total`: `delivery_time / total_fulfillment_time_min`.
- `high_rating_flag`: `rating_num >= 4` (missing/unknown when `rated_flag==False`).
- `top_rating_flag`: `rating_num == 5` (missing/unknown when `rated_flag==False`).

## Customer-level (`data/processed/customer_features.csv`)

Outcome/proxy targets:
- `n_orders`: total orders observed for the customer.
- `repeat_customer_flag`: `n_orders >= 2` (repeat-within-dataset proxy).

Experience / hypothesis drivers:
- `avg_delivery_time`, `std_delivery_time`, `delivery_time_cv`: delivery speed + consistency proxies.
- `avg_total_fulfillment_time`, `std_total_fulfillment_time`, `total_time_cv`: end-to-end speed + consistency proxies.
- `rated_share`, `avg_rating`, `pct_high_rating`, `pct_top_rating`: satisfaction proxies (selection bias possible due to unrated orders).
- `has_rated_orders`: whether the customer has at least one rated order (when false, `avg_rating` / `pct_*` are missing).
- `restaurant_variety`, `cuisine_variety`: variety / breadth-of-usage proxies.

Controls / descriptive:
- `weekend_share`, `weekday_share`: fraction of orders on weekends/weekdays.
- `avg_cost`, `median_cost`, `std_cost`: spend proxies.
- `avg_prep_time`, `std_prep_time`: kitchen prep-time proxies.
- `top_cuisine_type`, `top_restaurant_name`: most common cuisine/restaurant for the customer (mode).

## Restaurant-level (`data/processed/restaurant_features.csv`)

- `n_orders`, `n_customers`: volume and reach.
- `avg_delivery_time`, `std_delivery_time`, `delivery_time_cv`: delivery speed + consistency proxies.
- `avg_total_fulfillment_time`: end-to-end speed proxy.
- `rated_share`, `avg_rating`, `pct_high_rating`, `pct_top_rating`: satisfaction proxies.
- `has_rated_orders`: whether the restaurant has at least one rated order (when false, `avg_rating` / `pct_*` are missing).
- `avg_cost`, `avg_prep_time`, `weekend_share`, `cuisine_type`: descriptive/context.

## Cuisine-level (`data/processed/cuisine_features.csv`)

- `n_orders`, `n_restaurants`, `n_customers`: footprint by cuisine.
- `avg_cost`, `avg_total_fulfillment_time`, `weekend_share`, `weekday_share`: descriptive/context.
- `rated_share`, `avg_rating`: satisfaction proxies.
